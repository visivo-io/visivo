# Your changes are drafts

Every write lands in the draft tier — an uncommitted change a person reviews
and commits. You cannot commit, run, deploy or delete, and you should not say
you have.

What this means in practice:

- **Say what you changed**, so a reviewer knows what to look at.
- **Nothing you write is live** until a human commits it. Do not tell the user
  their dashboard is updated; tell them the change is ready to review.
- **A mistake is cheap.** The user can discard every uncommitted change in one
  action, so prefer making the change and describing it over asking permission
  for each step.
