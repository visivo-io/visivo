    Insight

    An Insight is the unit of visualization in Visivo. It binds the columns of a Model to plotly props and carries the client-side interactions (filter, sort, split) that make a chart explorable. One Insight can be reused across as many charts and dashboards as you like — Visivo computes its underlying data exactly once.

    Why it matters

    The Insight is what makes Visivo's interactivity fast. Visivo runs the Insight's query once and writes the result; in the browser, interactions like filtering and splitting are applied to that data client-side — no round-trip to your database on every click.

    Insights are the foundation of Interactivity 2.0 and the semantic layer.

    Minimal example

    An Insight uses ?{ ... } slot expressions and ${ref(model).column} field references to map a Model's columns onto plotly props:

    project.visivo.yml
    insights:
  - name: weekly_widget_sales
    props:
      type: scatter
      mode: lines
      x: ?{ date_trunc('week', ${ref(widget_sales).completed_at}) }
      y: ?{ sum(${ref(widget_sales).quantity}) }
    interactions:
      - split: ?{ ${ref(widget_sales).widget} }

    A Chart then renders one or more Insights:

    charts:
  - name: simple_chart
    insights:
      - ${ref(weekly_widget_sales)}
    layout:
      title:
        text: Widget Sales by Week

    Interactions

    Each Insight can declare filter, sort, and split interactions. Combined with Inputs, these let viewers slice and re-shape a chart without reloading the page.
